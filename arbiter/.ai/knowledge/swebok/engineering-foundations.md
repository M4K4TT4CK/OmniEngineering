# Engineering Foundations

This knowledge pack adapts engineering foundations for OmniContext. Use it when
a task requires disciplined problem solving, constraints, trade studies, safety
margins, lifecycle thinking, systems interaction, or evidence-based decisions.

## Purpose

Engineering foundations connect software work to broader engineering practice:
requirements, constraints, design tradeoffs, verification, risk, safety,
maintainability, and lifecycle responsibility.

In OmniContext, engineering foundations should help an LLM or human engineer:

- Treat software decisions as engineered tradeoffs.
- Identify constraints and failure modes.
- Use evidence before claiming success.
- Preserve lifecycle maintainability.
- Escalate high-impact risk.

## Engineering Habits

- Define the problem before solving it.
- Identify constraints and assumptions.
- Compare alternatives.
- Design for verification.
- Consider failure modes.
- Keep safety and security visible.
- Document decisions that future maintainers will need.
- Validate against requirements, not just implementation intent.

## Failure And Risk Thinking

For high-impact changes, ask:

- What can fail?
- How would we detect it?
- What is the consequence?
- How can we reduce likelihood or impact?
- What is the rollback or recovery plan?

## LLM Operating Rules

When engineering foundations matter:

1. Read `.ai/knowledge/swebok/engineering-foundations.md`.
2. Use `.ai/knowledge/swebok/engineering-foundations-checklist.md`.
3. State constraints, alternatives, risks, and validation evidence.
4. Escalate safety, security, destructive, or irreversible decisions.
5. Keep future maintenance cost visible.
