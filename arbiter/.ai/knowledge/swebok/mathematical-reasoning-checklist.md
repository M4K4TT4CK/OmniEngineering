# Mathematical Reasoning Checklist

Use this checklist when a task includes quantitative or formal claims.

- Units are stated.
- Assumptions are stated.
- Boundary cases are considered.
- Tolerances are defined for approximate behavior.
- Metrics are defined before interpretation.
- Sample size/source is known for statistical claims.
- Complexity claims are justified.
- No unsupported probability, causality, or confidence claim is made.
