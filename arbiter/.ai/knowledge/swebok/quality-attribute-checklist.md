# Quality Attribute Checklist

Use this checklist when quality is part of the requirement, risk, review, or
release decision.

- Functional correctness is addressed.
- Security impact is considered.
- Reliability and recovery impact are considered.
- Performance impact is considered.
- Usability impact is considered.
- Maintainability impact is considered.
- Testability impact is considered.
- Compatibility and portability impact are considered.
- Observability impact is considered.
- Evidence exists for each important quality claim.
