# Process Tailoring Checklist

Use this checklist to choose the right amount of process for a task.

- Requirement ID exists or is proposed.
- Task risk is classified as low, medium, or high.
- Relevant playbooks are selected.
- Relevant checklists are selected.
- Required validation is identified.
- Documentation and handoff needs are identified.
- Extra ceremony is avoided for low-risk changes.
- Additional review is required for high-risk changes.
