# Engineering Management Checklist

Use this checklist for multi-step, high-risk, or ambiguous engineering work.

## Scope

- Objective is stated.
- Requirement IDs are assigned.
- In-scope and out-of-scope work are clear.
- Minimum access scope is defined.

## Planning

- Work is split into manageable steps.
- Dependencies are identified.
- Validation plan is defined.
- Documentation impact is defined.
- Completion criteria are defined.

## Risk

- Technical risks are listed.
- Product or stakeholder risks are listed.
- Security, data, and operational risks are considered.
- Mitigations or assumptions are documented.

## Tracking

- Completed work is distinguishable from pending work.
- Blockers are explicit.
- Decisions are recorded.
- Handoff information is sufficient for another model or human.

## Completion

- Validation evidence exists or skipped checks are explained.
- Requirements and docs are updated according to project policy.
- Final report does not overclaim.
