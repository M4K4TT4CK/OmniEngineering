# Design Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before approving or implementing a meaningful design change.

## Traceability

- Design maps to one or more requirements.
- Acceptance criteria are known.
- Quality attributes are identified.
- Constraints and assumptions are documented.

## Structure

- Responsibilities are clear.
- Module boundaries are clear.
- Data ownership is clear.
- Dependency direction is intentional.
- Public interfaces do not expose unnecessary internal detail.

## Behavior

- Important flows are described.
- Error and edge cases are described.
- State transitions are valid and bounded.
- Compatibility impact is understood.

## Quality Attributes

- Security impact is considered.
- Performance impact is considered.
- Reliability and recovery impact are considered.
- Maintainability and testability are considered.
- Observability impact is considered when operations matter.

## Validation

- Design can be reviewed against requirements.
- Implementation can be tested.
- Required tests or checks are identified.
- Risks and tradeoffs are documented.
