# Model And Method Selection Checklist

Use this checklist before creating models or applying a structured method.

- Model or method purpose is clear.
- Requirement, design, risk, or validation question is identified.
- Audience is identified.
- Level of detail is appropriate.
- Model will be maintained or intentionally temporary.
- Notation is understandable to the project.
- Output links to requirements, design, tests, or decisions.
- Simpler representation was considered.
