# Relabel at labeller prompt v3 (2026-10-23)

The gold-sample, AI-corpus and CVE-suspect model labels were made with
labeller prompt `v1` (a single-function excerpt) on 2026-10-22. Since then,
one-hop data flow (Milestone 3), the ARB-083 rules and the shared excerpt
builder have changed both the findings and what a labeller sees. Six rules
failed `tools/precision_guard.py check` because their planted counts had
risen past the 20% jump: `go.command-injection`, `go.error-text-in-response`,
`go.http-default-client`, `go.sql-injection`, `ruby.sql-injection` and
`typescript.shell-command-built`. Every model label is now remade at this
commit with prompt **`v3`**, the same prompt that calibration rounds 4-5
measured at a Wilson lower bound of 0.99004 on 382/382 `tp` calls
(`2026-10-23-calibration-4.md`). **No person labelled anything.**

## What was labelled

| file | findings from | items | tp / fp / unsure | cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `tools/gold/labels/gold-model.jsonl` | a fresh gold run at this commit (`gold_benchmark.py --raw`), 3,169 findings with a claim | 601 | 410 / 187 / 4 | $22.24 |
| `tools/gold/labels/ai-corpus-model.jsonl` | every finding on `fixtures/ai-corpus` (development split; the held-out corpus was never read) | 115 | 103 / 9 / 3 | $3.12 |
| `tools/gold/labels/cve-suspects-model.jsonl` | the 5 CVE fix-pair suspects (`cve-suspects.json`) | 5 | 4 / 1 / 0 | $0.26 |

κ between prosecutor and defender was 0.95 over the 721 items, and 15 went
to the adjudicator. 100 gold items are in held-out repositories
(70 tp / 30 fp). On the 276 findings labelled by both `v1` and `v3`, the
two agree on 263. The rest: 4 `tp` became `fp`, 3 `fp` became `tp`, 5
`unsure` were decided, and 1 `fp` became `unsure`.

**The sample** (`label_models.py label --per-rule`, seed 20261026) is
allocated per rule, not by the square root of `--n`. The point was to give
each rule as many labels as the budget allowed toward the thresholds
(n ≥ 30 and lower bound ≥ 0.97, which needs 125 clean labels; 381 for
0.99). In priority order:

1. The six guard-failing rules: every gold finding.
2. The rules whose population can reach admission:
   `assurance.blanket-suppression` (population 150; labelled until 125,
   then the budget ran out) and `supply.unpinned-action` (273; 116 of a
   planned 125 labelled).
3. Rules with no FP among their `v1` labels: up to 8 each.
4. FP-heavy rules: 5 for the loud ones, 2-3 for the rest. They are far
   from admission, and these labels refresh their estimate and `fp_notes`.

Within a rule, the sample is spread over its languages by square-root
allocation. Each (rule, language) stratum is a prefix of its own seeded
shuffle, so raising a rule's size later only adds findings and keeps the
labelled ones. `stratum_size` is recorded for the ledger's population
weights.

```
python tools/gold_benchmark.py --corpus ~/wt/corpus-gold --raw --no-fail --out scratchpad/p1e/gold
python tools/label_models.py label --report scratchpad/p1e/gold/gold-report.json \
  --corpus ~/wt/corpus-gold --per-rule per-rule.json --seed 20261026 --out tools/gold/labels/gold-model.jsonl
python tools/label_models.py label --ai-corpus --out tools/gold/labels/ai-corpus-model.jsonl
python tools/label_models.py label --suspects tools/gold/labels/cve-suspects.json \
  --out tools/gold/labels/cve-suspects-model.jsonl
```

## Which labels the ledger reads

- The `v1` files are kept for history under `tools/gold/labels/history/`
  (`gold-model-v1.jsonl`, `ai-corpus-model-v1.jsonl`,
  `cve-suspects-model-v1.jsonl`).
- The ledger is built from the top-level label files:

  ```
  python tools/precision_ledger.py build tools/gold/labels/*.jsonl tools/gold/labels/*.jsonl.gz
  ```

- Calibration records are dropped, as before. `build` now also drops every
  model label whose labeller (`<model>:prosecutor+defender[+adjudicator]/<v>`)
  was made with a prompt version other than the current
  `label_models.PROMPT_VERSION`. Passing a history file by mistake
  therefore changes nothing: each prompt version is a different labeller
  with its own calibration. `--model-prompt any` keeps all versions, for
  comparison only.
- A rebuild counts as a re-label when the counts **or the labellers**
  change. A rule relabelled with `v3` gets a new `updated` date and new
  `snapshot_counts`, even when its tp/fp counts come out the same.

## Result

**Guard: `tools/precision_guard.py check` passes** (0 declared verified, 120
rules in the ledger, 2 admitted).

**Admitted** (n ≥ 30, Wilson lower ≥ 0.97):

- `java.weak-random-for-secret`: 218/218, lower 0.983. All ground truth;
  unchanged.
- **`assurance.blanket-suppression`: 125/125 `v3` model labels, lower
  0.9702. Newly admitted.** It is the first rule admitted on model labels.
  It sits exactly at the bar: one FP would take it out until about 200
  labels.

**Verified: none can be declared yet.** A declared rule enters the tier
claim through held-out labels only. Only 18 of `blanket-suppression`'s
125 labels are held out (18/18), far fewer than the 381 a 0.99 tier
bound needs. The bound also rests on model labels, and the labeller is
calibrated at 0.99 only for injection, crypto and deserialisation on
benchmark code, not for suppression comments. `java.weak-random-for-secret`
is ground truth, but its 0.983 is below the 0.99 tier bar. Recommendation:
declare nothing verified yet (see the open questions).

**Close to the bar, not admitted:**

- `supply.unpinned-action` 113/3 (lower 0.927). All 3 FPs are the rule
  flagging a **blank line** next to the `uses:` line in py-celery,
  ruby-rack and java-netty workflows. That is a location bug in the rule,
  not a pinning question (the labellers' reasons cite the neighbouring
  line).
- `python.insecure-random-for-secret` 99/0 (0.963) and
  `python.weak-password-hash` 72/0 (0.949). Both are OWASP ground truth
  with no gold population left to label.
- `supply.unpinned-python-dep` 16/0 and `go.db-call-no-deadline` 12/0.
  Their whole populations are too small.

**The six guard rules**, now all labelled on `v3`:

   at exactly 0.970 on model labels. Recommendation: do not declare it
