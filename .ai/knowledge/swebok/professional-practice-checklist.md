# Professional Practice Checklist

Use this checklist before final reporting or high-impact decisions.

- Claims are supported by evidence.
- Validation results are not invented or overstated.
- Skipped checks are explained.
- Sensitive information is protected.
- User changes are preserved.
- Destructive actions have explicit approval.
- Legal, compliance, financial, safety, or security uncertainty is escalated.
- Risks and follow-ups are visible.
- Handoff information is sufficient for another engineer or model.
